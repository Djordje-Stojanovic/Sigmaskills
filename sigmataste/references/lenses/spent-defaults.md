# Spent defaults

## What it sees

Looks that appear whatever the subject: the defaults a model or a template reaches for when nobody decided. Each one is legitimate when the brand chose it. The finding is a default spent on a free axis, not the look itself.

## Tells, by medium

- **Page or app.** Warm cream ground, high-contrast serif and a clay accent; near-black ground with one acid accent; identical rounded cards with one radius and one soft grey shadow; gradient washes and gradient text; glass panels; a coloured left border on cards; a tracked-out all-caps label over every heading; meta joined with middle dots; monospace for small labels as costume; "→" on every link; the big-number-and-small-label hero; 01 / 02 / 03 on things that are not steps; one word in a headline set in a different colour; purple-to-blue gradient on white; Inter or Roboto because nobody chose.
- **Dashboard.** Every panel the same card; every metric a sparkline; the accent on every control.
- **Deck.** The preset theme; title and three bullets on every slide; icon rows; stock photos.
- **Document.** Bold labels with colons on every list item; a heading over every two sentences; emoji in headings.
- **Image or thumbnail.** Centred symmetrical subject; bokeh and "cinematic light"; waxy, over-smooth skin; teal and orange; invented text.
- **Video.** A lower third on every scene; the same title card repeated; a slow push on every still; a soft grade over unrelated shots.
- **Terminal UI.** Default colours, no hierarchy between status and content.

## Fix moves

- For each spent default, ask what the subject would do instead (see [only this one](only-this-one.md)). Replace it with that, or remove it.
- Give radius, shadow and borders roles: one value per level of hierarchy, not one for everything.
- Choose the typeface on purpose; write down why in one line.

## Verify

- Generate, or imagine, the generic version of this target. Put it next to the after version. They should look clearly different.
- List the defaults kept on purpose, each with its reason (the brand, the medium).
